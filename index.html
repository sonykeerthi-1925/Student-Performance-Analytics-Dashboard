Conclusion

The analysis shows a positive association between study time and final academic performance.

Students in higher study-time categories generally showed higher average final grades.

Previous period grades (G1 and G2) showed strong positive correlations with final grades (G3).

These findings describe patterns in the dataset and do not establish cause-and-effect relationships.

Study Limitations

  • The analysis uses data from 649 students.
  • The dataset represents two Portuguese secondary schools.
  • Observational data cannot establish causation.
  • The findings may not generalize to all student populations.
